Dear Editor,

Please consider our Original Paper, “Benchmarking a Structured Probabilistic Decision Model on Nine Years of the Japanese Medical Licensing Examination: Accuracy, Missing-Visual-Information Robustness, Calibration, Selective Prediction, Latency, and Cost,” by Ren Matsushita, MD (Independent Researcher).

This study evaluates a structured probabilistic decision model on 3,556 option-choice questions derived from the 112th–120th Japanese Medical Licensing Examinations. Rather than focusing only on whether an AI system exceeds a passing threshold, we characterize performance across graded image dependence, native per-option probabilities, selective prediction, wall-clock API latency, cost, and conservative examination-by-examination score reconstruction. The principal contribution is therefore an analysis of uncertainty and information-dependence in a decision-only model, not a claim of clinical competence.
